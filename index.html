    Distance

    Worked from the chart’s own output. The difference in sun time is an angle at 15° to the hour. Taken with the two latitudes it gives the angle between the two places, and Gleason’s sixty geographical miles to the degree turns that into a distance. Haversine is used to verify the consistency of the time conversion to geographical miles. A straight ruler laid across the chart is not this, because a line that misses the centre is not on a radial.
